About us · Afritech Sports Technology and Innovation GroupÀ propos · Afritech Sports Technology and Innovation GroupKuhusu sisi · Afritech Sports Technology and Innovation GroupSobre nós · Afritech Sports Technology and Innovation Group

Built in Nairobi for African football.Créé à Nairobi pour le football africain.Imejengwa Nairobi kwa ajili ya soka la Afrika.Feito em Nairóbi para o futebol africano.

We are a football intelligence and sports technology company. We help clubs, academies, agents and federations across Africa find talent, read the game and prove what their players are worth, with match analysis, scouting dossiers, football CVs and player tracking.

Nous sommes une société d’intelligence football et de technologie sportive. Nous aidons clubs, académies, agents et fédérations d’Afrique à trouver les talents, lire le jeu et prouver la valeur de leurs joueurs, grâce à l’analyse de match, aux dossiers de recrutement, aux CV football et au suivi des joueurs.

Sisi ni kampuni ya ujasusi wa soka na teknolojia ya michezo. Tunasaidia klabu, akademia, mawakala na mashirikisho kote Afrika kupata vipaji, kusoma mchezo na kuthibitisha thamani ya wachezaji wao, kwa uchambuzi wa mechi, ripoti za uskauti, CV za soka na ufuatiliaji wa wachezaji.

Somos uma empresa de inteligência de futebol e tecnologia esportiva. Ajudamos clubes, academias, agentes e federações da África a encontrar talentos, ler o jogo e provar o valor dos seus jogadores, com análise de partidas, dossiês de scouting, currículos de futebol e monitoramento.

Why we existPourquoi nous existonsKwa nini tupoPor que existimos

Africa doesn’t lack talent. It lacks evidence.L’Afrique ne manque pas de talent. Elle manque de preuves.Afrika haikosi vipaji. Inakosa ushahidi.Falta à África não o talento, mas as evidências.

Players across Africa perform every weekend in front of no scouts, no cameras and no data. When one of them moves, the clubs that raised him are owed training rewards, but only if they can prove it. We build the match video, the numbers and the records that make that proof possible.

Partout en Afrique, des joueurs jouent chaque week-end sans recruteurs, sans caméras et sans données. Quand l’un d’eux est transféré, les clubs formateurs ont droit à des indemnités, à condition de le prouver. Nous produisons la vidéo, les chiffres et les dossiers qui rendent cette preuve possible.

Wachezaji kote Afrika wanacheza kila wikendi bila maskauti, bila kamera na bila data. Mmoja wao anapohama, klabu zilizomkuza zinastahili fidia ya mafunzo, lakini tu kama zinaweza kuthibitisha. Tunatengeneza video, takwimu na kumbukumbu zinazowezesha uthibitisho huo.

Jogadores de toda a África jogam todo fim de semana sem olheiros, sem câmeras e sem dados. Quando um deles é transferido, os clubes formadores têm direito a compensações, mas só se provarem. Nós produzimos o vídeo, os números e os registros que tornam essa prova possível.
